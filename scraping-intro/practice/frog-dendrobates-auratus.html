Green and black poison dart frog

Dendrobates auratus

A brightly coloured poison dart frog with green or blue markings on a dark background. It is one of the most variable poison dart frogs.

Family
Dendrobatidae
Genus
Dendrobates
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Southern Central America and Colombia; introduced to Oahu, Hawaii.
