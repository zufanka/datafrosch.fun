Dyeing poison dart frog

Dendrobates tinctorius

A large, variably coloured poison dart frog, usually blue and yellow or black. It lives on the forest floor and uses water held by fallen trees for tadpoles.

Family
Dendrobatidae
Genus
Dendrobates
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Eastern Guiana Shield, including French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname and Brazil.
