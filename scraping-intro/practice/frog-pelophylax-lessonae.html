Pool frog

Pelophylax lessonae

A small green frog of shallow, still water. It hibernates in woodland soil and can survive being frozen overnight.

Family
Ranidae
Genus
Pelophylax
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Much of Europe, from France east to Russia.
