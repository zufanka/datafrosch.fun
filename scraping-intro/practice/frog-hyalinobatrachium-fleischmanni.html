Fleischmann's glass frog

Hyalinobatrachium fleischmanni

A small green frog with translucent skin on its belly. It lives in lowland and mid-elevation forests and calls during the rainy season.

Family
Centrolenidae
Genus
Hyalinobatrachium
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Tropical Americas, from southern Mexico to Ecuador.
