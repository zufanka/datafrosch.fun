Northern leopard frog

Lithobates pipiens

A spotted green-brown frog with dark blotches and a pale ridge along each side. It is the state amphibian of Minnesota and Vermont.

Family
Ranidae
Genus
Lithobates
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Parts of Canada and the United States.
