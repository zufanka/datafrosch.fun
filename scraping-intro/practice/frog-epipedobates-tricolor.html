Phantasmal poison frog

Epipedobates tricolor

A small poison dart frog with warm colours and light stripes. It is endemic to a few locations in Ecuador and is classified as vulnerable.

Family
Dendrobatidae
Genus
Epipedobates
Conservation status
Vulnerable (IUCN Red List)
Range
Andean slopes of central Ecuador, in Bolívar Province.
