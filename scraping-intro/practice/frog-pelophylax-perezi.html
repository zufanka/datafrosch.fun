Perez's frog

Pelophylax perezi

An Iberian water frog, usually green or brown, common in ponds and streams. It has been introduced to the Canary Islands, Madeira, the Azores and the United Kingdom.

Family
Ranidae
Genus
Pelophylax
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Spain, Portugal and southern France; introduced to several islands.
