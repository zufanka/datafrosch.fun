Pacific tree frog

Pseudacris regilla

A small frog that can be green or brown and change colour over hours or weeks. It lives from sea level to high mountains.

Family
Hylidae
Genus
Pseudacris
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Pacific Northwest, from northern California to British Columbia and southern Alaska.
