Frog directory

A practice directory of 30 real frog species, ten per page across three pages. The listing gives you names and links; each frog’s family, conservation status, range and photo are on its detail page.

Page 1 of 3 · 10 frogs per page · 30 frogs in total

  1. Common frog

    Rana temporaria

    A semi-aquatic frog with a plump body, long hind legs and a brown, spotted back. It breeds in shallow ponds in spring.

  2. American bullfrog

    Lithobates catesbeianus

    A large green or brown frog with a deep, bull-like call. It lives in ponds, lakes and slow rivers and eats a wide range of prey.

  3. Spring peeper

    Pseudacris crucifer

    A small chorus frog with a cross-shaped mark on its back. Its chirping call is one of the first signs of spring.

  4. Red-eyed tree frog

    Agalychnis callidryas

    A bright green tree frog with red eyes, blue and yellow side stripes and orange feet. It is not poisonous; the colours help it hide and startle predators.

  5. Australian green tree frog

    Pelodryas caerulea

    A large green tree frog with a rounded head, often found near houses and other human dwellings. It is also called White's tree frog or the dumpy tree frog.

  6. Golden poison frog

    Phyllobates terribilis

    A small golden-orange frog endemic to Colombia. It is considered one of the most toxic animals in the world.

  7. Goliath frog

    Conraua goliath

    The largest living frog. It lives near fast-flowing rivers in dense tropical forest.

  8. Southern corroboree frog

    Pseudophryne corroboree

    A small yellow-and-black ground frog native to southeastern Australia. It is critically endangered.

  9. Wallace's flying frog

    Rhacophorus nigropalmatus

    A moss frog with large webbed feet that help it glide between trees. Alfred Russel Wallace collected the first known specimen.

  10. Cranwell's horned frog

    Ceratophrys cranwelli

    A wide-mouthed terrestrial frog, often called the Pac-man frog. Adults usually measure 8–13 cm and can weigh up to 0.5 kg.
