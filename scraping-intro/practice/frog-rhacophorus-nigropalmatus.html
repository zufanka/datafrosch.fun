Wallace's flying frog

Rhacophorus nigropalmatus

A moss frog with large webbed feet that help it glide between trees. Alfred Russel Wallace collected the first known specimen.

Family
Rhacophoridae
Genus
Rhacophorus
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
Malay Peninsula, western Indonesia, southern Thailand, Borneo and Sumatra.
