Frog directory

A practice directory of 30 real frog species, ten per page across three pages. The listing gives you names and links; each frog’s family, conservation status, range and photo are on its detail page.

Page 3 of 3 · 10 frogs per page · 30 frogs in total

  1. Agile frog

    Rana dalmatina

    A slender brown frog with unusually long hind legs. It lives in open deciduous woodland and can jump up to two metres.

  2. Marsh frog

    Pelophylax ridibundus

    A large green water frog that lives in ponds, lakes and rivers, including brackish coastal water. Males produce loud calls.

  3. Pool frog

    Pelophylax lessonae

    A small green frog of shallow, still water. It hibernates in woodland soil and can survive being frozen overnight.

  4. Perez's frog

    Pelophylax perezi

    An Iberian water frog, usually green or brown, common in ponds and streams. It has been introduced to the Canary Islands, Madeira, the Azores and the United Kingdom.

  5. Green and golden bell frog

    Ranoidea aurea

    A ground-dwelling tree frog with green and gold markings. It can reach 11 cm and usually stays close to the ground.

  6. Dyeing poison dart frog

    Dendrobates tinctorius

    A large, variably coloured poison dart frog, usually blue and yellow or black. It lives on the forest floor and uses water held by fallen trees for tadpoles.

  7. Strawberry poison dart frog

    Oophaga pumilio

    A small poison dart frog with many colour forms, with about 15–30 described morphs. It lives in humid lowland and premontane forest.

  8. Green and black poison dart frog

    Dendrobates auratus

    A brightly coloured poison dart frog with green or blue markings on a dark background. It is one of the most variable poison dart frogs.

  9. Phantasmal poison frog

    Epipedobates tricolor

    A small poison dart frog with warm colours and light stripes. It is endemic to a few locations in Ecuador and is classified as vulnerable.

  10. Fleischmann's glass frog

    Hyalinobatrachium fleischmanni

    A small green frog with translucent skin on its belly. It lives in lowland and mid-elevation forests and calls during the rainy season.
