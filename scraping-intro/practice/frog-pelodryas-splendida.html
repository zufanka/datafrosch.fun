Magnificent tree frog

Pelodryas splendida

A large green tree frog with white spots, first described in 1977. It has a small range along Australia's north-western coast.

Family
Pelodryadidae
Genus
Pelodryas
Conservation status
Least Concern (IUCN Red List)
Range
North-western Australia, in the Northern Territory and the Kimberley.
